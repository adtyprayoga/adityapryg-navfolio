Hi, I'm Aditya Prayoga. Mas, Adit, or Yoga, depending on who is asking.

I'm a software engineer in Jakarta, Indonesia. Since 2019 I have built and
maintained national-scale government platforms: personnel systems, online
submissions, certification, environmental data, and logistics.

Most of that work is modular **Laravel** architecture, role-based access with
audit and event logging, and performance tuning on the paths that carry load. I
do the systems analysis alongside it, turning BPMN and UML into a backlog you
can test against. I teach IT courses, and I hold ISTQB CTFL.
