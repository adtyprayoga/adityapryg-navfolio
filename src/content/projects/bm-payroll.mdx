**Tech Lead at BraindevsID · 2025 · Brainmatics**

Indonesian payroll, end to end. PPh 21 under the PP 58/2023 TER method, BPJS
health and employment contributions, seven allowance types, and a period that
moves from draft to calculated to approved to disbursed.

Tax brackets, TER rates, PTKP values, and BPJS rates sit in the database rather
than in code. Indonesian rates change by regulation, and a payroll run has to
reproduce the numbers that applied when it ran, not the ones current today.
Gross-up allowances take an iterative solve, since the tax on an allowance moves
the allowance.

Calculation is queued per employee, and Reverb pushes batch progress to the
browser. Laravel 12 and PostgreSQL behind Inertia and React, Octane on
FrankenPHP, Redis for cache and queues, Pest for the tests, PHPStan at level 5.
