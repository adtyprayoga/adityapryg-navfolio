**Senior Programmer at BrainmaticsID · 2021–2023 · KORBINMAS POLRI**

A multi-agency submissions platform. Digitizing the workflow and automating
verification brought end-to-end processing down from roughly three months to
three weeks.

The submission flow reads from several external government systems over REST —
OSS, BNPB, and the Polri Data Lake — so verification happens against
authoritative sources instead of by hand.

Laravel and PostgreSQL, with performance work on the reporting paths.
