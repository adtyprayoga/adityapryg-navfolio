**Senior Programmer at BrainmaticsID · 2021–2023 · KORBINMAS POLRI**

A multi-agency submissions platform. We digitized the workflow and automated
verification, which brought end-to-end processing down from roughly three months
to three weeks.

The submission flow reads from several external government systems over REST API:
OSS, BNPB, and the Polri Data Lake. Verification runs against those sources
instead of by hand.

Laravel and PostgreSQL, with performance work on the reporting paths.
