Hi, I'm Aditya Prayoga — Mas, Adit, or Yoga, depending on who is asking.

I'm a software engineer based in Jakarta, Indonesia, with five years of building
and maintaining national-scale government platforms: personnel systems, online
submissions, certification, environmental data, and logistics.

Most of that work is modular **Laravel** architecture, role-based access with
audit and event logging, and performance work on the paths that matter — with
system analysis alongside it, turning BPMN and UML into a backlog you can
actually test against. I also teach IT courses, and I hold ISTQB CTFL.
