**System Analyst · 2020 · Kementerian Sosial RI**


I designed SIKS-NG in full: use case diagrams and their supporting detail
(sequence, activity, ERD, and data model diagrams), then the software
requirements specification and the accompanying terms of reference for Sistem
Informasi Kesejahteraan Sosial Next Generation, the social welfare information
system.

I did not build it. The design and the specification were the deliverable, and
the build that followed was measured against them.
