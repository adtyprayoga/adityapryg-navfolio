**Tech Lead at BrainmaticsID · 2023 · BRIN**

A national lake information platform, pulling authoritative datasets that had
been scattered across agencies into one place so conservation policy and
long-term environmental planning could be argued from shared evidence.

Led the build: Laravel for the backend and data services, Next.js for the
public portal, PostgreSQL underneath.
