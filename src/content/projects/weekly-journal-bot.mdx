A weekly reporting workflow for teams that already submit journals in Telegram.
The bot identifies the sender and reporting group, checks the journal format and
submission window, and stores structured reports in **Google Sheets**.

It sends a Friday reminder and a Saturday recap, while keeping the report status
and weekly and monthly summaries deterministic. Employees can use a private
Telegram menu for guidance; reports are submitted in their assigned group topic.

Built with **Python**, **FastAPI**, **gspread**, and **Docker** for deployment on
Coolify.
