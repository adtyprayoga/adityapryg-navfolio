**Programmer · 2022 · Korbinmas Baharkam Polri**

SISLAP is the Sistem Pelaporan module inside
[Binmas Online System V2](/projects/bos-v2). Bhabinkamtibmas officers, Polsus,
and operators across Polsek, Polres, and Polda file their daily activity reports
through it, and command staff read the situation from that same data instead of
waiting on a paper trail.

I worked on the reporting side. Taking it off paper also gave the directorate a
channel it reused to collect Covid-19 vaccination data.
